Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题解答 (FAQ)

1. Does INRange calculate or change my medication dosage?
1. INRange 会自动计算或调整我的药量吗？
No. INRange is strictly a logging, tracking, and reminder tool. It never prescribes or adjusts doses. You must enter the exact dose schedule prescribed by your physician or anticoagulation clinic.
不会。INRange 仅作为服药记录与日程提醒辅助工具，绝不自行计算或更改处方剂量。您在应用中设置的每日服药量必须完全遵循主管医师或抗凝门诊医嘱。
2. How does iCloud synchronization work?
2. iCloud 云同步是如何运作的？
When signed into your Apple ID with iCloud Drive enabled, INRange automatically syncs your INR history and dosage schedule across your iPhone devices through Apple's secure CloudKit Private Database.
只要您的 iPhone 登录了 Apple ID 并开启 iCloud，INRange 会自动通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 私有云数据库同步您的服药历史与 INR 数据，保障数据安全与多设备一致。
3. How do I export records to show my physician?
3. 如何将记录导出给主治医生看？
Navigate to Settings → tap Export Doctor Report. You can generate a formatted PDF report or copy raw summary text to share via AirDrop, Email, or Print.
进入 设置 (Settings) → 点击 导出就医报告，系统会自动生成格式严谨的 PDF 报告或纯文本摘要，支持一键通过隔空投送、微信、邮件或打印分享给医生。
4. Can I customize pill tablet specifications?
4. 可以自定义药片规格（单片毫克数）吗？
Yes! Go to Settings → Pill Specification. You can choose common specs like 2.5mg, 3.0mg, or 5.0mg, and the tablet breakdown graphics will immediately update to reflect full and partial tablets.
支持！在 设置 → 单片药量规格 中，您可以选择 2.5mg、3.0mg 或 5.0mg 等常见华法林片规格，药片图形分解会自动按该规格呈现整片与分片。
