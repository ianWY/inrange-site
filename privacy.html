Zero Data Collection 零数据收集

1. Personal Data and Health Privacy

1. 个人数据与健康隐私

At INRange, we believe your health records belong strictly to you. INRange does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your personal health data, medication dosage schedules, or INR checkup logs to developer servers or any third-party marketing companies.

在 INRange，我们坚信您的健康与用药数据完全属于您自己。INRange 不会收集、传输、存储、出售或共享您的任何个人健康数据、用药日程或 INR 检查记录到开发者服务器或任何第三方商业机构。

The application operates on an offline-first architecture. All records you create reside solely on your device.

本应用基于“离线优先 (Offline-First)”理念架构设计。您创建的所有记录均直接保存在您的本地设备上。

2. Apple iCloud & CloudKit Sync

2. Apple iCloud 与 CloudKit 同步

If you enable Apple iCloud on your device, INRange synchronizes your data across your personal Apple devices using Apple's encrypted CloudKit Private Database.

若您在设备上启用了 Apple iCloud，INRange 将借助 Apple 原生的 CloudKit 私有数据库 (Private Database) 在您的个人 Apple 设备之间进行端到端加密同步。

  • Your data is stored within your own private iCloud account storage container.
  • 您的数据仅保存在您个人的 iCloud 私有存储空间中。
  • Neither the developer of INRange nor any unauthorized entity has access to your private database or encryption keys.
  • 无论是 INRange 开发者还是任何第三方均无法访问您的私有数据库或读取未加密数据。

3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

3. 第三方分析与追踪技术

INRange contains zero third-party tracking frameworks, zero advertising SDKs, and zero behavioral analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or Flurry).

INRange 绝不嵌入任何第三方追踪框架、广告 SDK 或行为统计分析库（例如 Google Analytics、Firebase、Facebook SDK 等）。

We do not track your location, do not read your identifier for advertisers (IDFA), and do not record your browsing habits.

我们不读取您的广告标识符 (IDFA)，不追踪您的地理位置，也不记录您的使用偏好习惯。

4. Local Notifications

4. 本地通知功能

INRange utilizes Apple's native UserNotifications framework to deliver daily dosage reminders and upcoming INR checkup alerts. All notifications are scheduled and triggered strictly locally on your iOS device without sending any data over remote notification networks.

INRange 使用 Apple 原生 UserNotifications 框架为您发送每日服药提醒及下次验血复查提示。所有通知日程均由本地设备操作系统触发，绝不将用药内容上传到任何远程外部推送系统。

5. Medical Disclaimer

5. 医疗免责声明

INRange is a personal medication tracking and reminder tool. It is not a medical device, nor a diagnostic tool. INRange does not calculate, prescribe, or alter warfarin dosages. Always consult your attending physician, cardiologist, or anticoagulation specialist for medical instructions.

INRange 仅作为个人服药打卡与数值记录辅助工具。本软件不是医疗器械，亦不具备临床诊断或处方计算能力。 软件绝不自动建议或调整华法林用药量，所有剂量均应严格遵照主管医师、心血管专科医生或抗凝门诊的医嘱执行。
